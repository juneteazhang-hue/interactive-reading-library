## 八句连读版

1. Drake worked in the onion field and expected to stay a farmer.
2. A royal soldier called Drake and waited on a black horse.
3. Drake saw the king's golden dragon symbol and was pulled onto the horse.
4. The soldier said the king had chosen Drake, so his mother told him to obey.
5. They crossed the village and bridge until the castle appeared ahead.
6. The soldier led Drake downstairs and left him beside a stone door.
7. Drake saw dragon marks and warm light, so he opened the door.
8. A giant red dragon breathed fire, and Drake's ordinary life changed forever.
