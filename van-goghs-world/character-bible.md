# Van Gogh's World Visual Bible

## Vincent identity lock

- Dutch male artist with a lean, angular face, pale skin, a long straight nose, high cheekbones, blue-green eyes, and a thoughtful, emotionally open gaze.
- Adult continuity (scenes 1 and 3–8, plus the cover): short swept-back red hair, a neat red beard and moustache, and the same recognizable facial proportions. Scenes 3–8 show a subtle progression from early to late thirties without changing the identity.
- Scene 2 is the approved younger form: the same eyes, nose, cheekbones, and hairline, with a clean-shaven face and softer youthful features.
- Signature clothing: late-19th-century dark teal work jacket over an ochre waistcoat and cream collarless shirt. Garments may look more worn in later scenes, but their colors and period construction stay recognizable.
- Expression is humane and restrained, never comic, grotesque, or caricatured.

## Shared visual language

- Cinematic animated biographical film fused with expressive post-impressionist oil texture.
- Vivid cobalt blue and deep indigo balanced by sunflower yellow and burning orange.
- Swirling short brushstrokes, tactile impasto, volumetric light, painterly depth, and clearly staged silhouettes.
- Historically grounded late-19th-century European interiors, streets, clothing, furniture, tools, and landscapes. No modern objects.
- Emotionally respectful throughout. Never show graphic injury, blood, an ear wound, a blade, a weapon, a body, or a reenactment of self-harm.
- No text, letters, captions, signatures, logos, watermarks, frames, borders, or baked-in typography.

## Composition contract

- Reading scenes are authored as 3:2 landscape compositions and finalized at 1440 × 960.
- Keep important faces, hands, and story objects away from the crop edges.
- Preserve calm, low-detail negative space around the two quiz-bubble centers recorded in the book data. Odd-numbered scenes reserve approximately `(28%, 42%)` and `(74%, 72%)`; even-numbered scenes reserve approximately `(74%, 42%)` and `(28%, 72%)`.
- The cover is authored as a 2:3 portrait movie-poster composition and finalized at 960 × 1440, with an uncluttered lower area for HTML overlays.

## Scene continuity arc

1. Adult Vincent amid symbolic motifs of worldwide recognition; this is the master face, hair, clothing, palette, and medium reference.
2. Younger, clean-shaven Vincent drawing in rural Zundert; identity remains recognizable through eyes, nose, cheekbones, and swept-back red hair.
3. Early-thirties Vincent in Paris, discovering vivid complementary color.
4. Mid-thirties Vincent alone in Arles at night; strain is conveyed through posture and light only.
5. Slightly older Vincent in a calm Saint-Rémy hospital-room studio, restored enough to paint.
6. Late-thirties Vincent working urgently outdoors in Auvers.
7. Vincent is absent; his folded signature-colored coat and studio objects carry his presence symbolically.
8. Vincent appears only as a translucent artistic presence whose adult face and clothing match scene 1.
